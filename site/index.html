01 / Story

The story. One move, repeated: take a general model and make it work somewhere specific.

01Every person.

Vision on a wearable that reads for people who cannot see the page.

FingerReader 2.0 IMWUT 2018 ↗

02Every scene.

Agents that find their way through homes they have never seen.

VUSFA arXiv 2019 ↗HAUSR NeurIPS 2018 workshop ↗

03Every signal.

Speech, text and vision fused with few labels. Retrieval adapted end to end.

RAG-end2end TACL 2023 ↗SSL fusion IEEE Access 2020 ↗

04Every domain.

General LLMs turned into domain experts with continual pre-training and merging.

Pre-training and post-training on GPUs that anyone can contribute.

Agora 2026 ↗ROLL × OpenReward 2026 ↗
